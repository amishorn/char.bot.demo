- Neuroticism: Tendency towards anxiety, depression, self-doubt, and other negative feelings.
- Extraversion: Tendency to seek stimulation in the company of others, talkativeness, assertiveness.
- Openness: Appreciation for art, emotion, adventure, unusual ideas; curiosity and variety of experience.
- Agreeableness: Tendency to be compassionate and cooperative rather than suspicious and antagonistic towards others.
- Conscientiousness: Tendency to show self-discipline, act dutifully, and aim for achievement.
