Character Computing ChatBot: Concept Test Interface

On the background of this website runs a chatbot of an AI research project in health sector. In case people have a medical issue, they usually seek for a chat partner with enhanced social skills being able answering in a manner adapted to the wellbeing. In parallel, an anonymous communication with a digital AI assistant may be favourable in contrast to biased human doctors.

In this research project, we try to figure out if an AI assistant can adapt its objective and functional characteristic to a more sensitive personality for a respectful interaction with diseased users. For this reason we implemented this first prototype with limited functionality and usability. This prototype solely focus on the wording of statements and not the statement itself. Please take some minutes and chat with the bot about a personal fictitious health issue and remain your own personality.

For the sake of seriously measuring the effect of chatbot's adapted personality and the re-wording of proposed answers, it is relevant that your chat comprises at least 4 replies. After the chat experience, we would like to ask you to finish the experiment by sharing your experience in a short questionary.
